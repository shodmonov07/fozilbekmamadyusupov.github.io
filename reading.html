Reading

I love reading books in seven directions and have turned it into a habit. Most importantly, a person should live by what they read.

01 — History

Understanding the past, people, civilizations and the events that shaped the present.

02 — Religion

Faith, knowledge, character, ethics and the search for a meaningful life.

03 — Law

Justice, rights, responsibility, legal systems and the principles that organize society.

04 — Personal Development

Discipline, habits, learning, self-awareness and continuous improvement.

05 — Literature

Stories, language, human experience and the ideas that literature preserves.

06 — Business

Entrepreneurship, trade, value creation, responsibility and practical thinking.

07 — Quotes

Short thoughts and words that can become reminders for action and reflection.

Books

Instruction of the Student: The Method of Learning

Burhan al-Din al-Zarnuji

A classic work about the discipline of learning, the character of a student, intention, teachers, knowledge and the habits that make learning meaningful.

The Mentor of Merchants

Ahadquli Kholmuhammad ugli

A work centered on business, trade, personal conduct and practical lessons for people who want to build themselves through honest work.

The Value of Time in the Eyes of Scholars

Shaykh Abd al-Fattah Abu Ghuddah

A reminder that time is one of the most valuable resources a person possesses and that serious people protect it through discipline and purposeful action.

The Harms of the Tongue

Imam Abu Hamid al-Ghazali

A reflection on speech, character, self-control and the responsibility that comes with the words we choose to say.

Atomic Habits

James Clear

Small, consistent improvements can compound over time. The book focuses on building useful systems and making good habits easier to maintain.

The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People

Stephen R. Covey

A framework for personal responsibility, principles, priorities, relationships and long-term effectiveness.

The Affairs of the World

Utkir Hoshimov

A literary work exploring family, humanity, everyday life and the emotions hidden inside ordinary experiences.

Deep Work

Cal Newport

A practical argument for focused, distraction-free work and the ability to develop valuable skills in a world full of interruptions.
