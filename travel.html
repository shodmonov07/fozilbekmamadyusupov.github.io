Travel

Every country leaves something behind — a lesson, a feeling, or a different way of seeing the world. Here are a few of mine.

A lesson for life

Latvia

I learned a great lesson here — one I will carry with me for the rest of my life. A good one, of course!

Finding balance

Sweden

When I first arrived in Sweden, the very first thing I noticed was how incredibly calm everyone seemed to be. No one appeared to be rushing. Everything worked like clockwork, yet there was hardly any chaos.

There is a Swedish concept called lagom, which roughly means “just the right amount.” You can see it in the way people dress, in the architecture, and in the general mindset. There is no need for flashy displays; simplicity is enough.

My absolute favorite part was fika — the tradition of taking a break to enjoy coffee and a conversation. Sweden showed me a different way to think about work-life balance and reminded me that life should be about more than constantly rushing forward.

The beauty of peace

Finland

Everyone calls Finland the happiest country in the world. But when you actually go there, you do not necessarily see people wearing constant smiles or celebrating all the time. You begin to understand that happiness can come from something much quieter: peace.

Finns have a deep connection with nature. Even in the middle of a city, you can feel as though you are walking through a forest. I really appreciated the respect for personal space and the comfort people seem to find in a quieter way of living.

And sitting outside in the crisp air after a traditional Finnish sauna? It felt like all the mental noise could finally switch off. Finland struck me as the perfect place to slow down, breathe, and reset.

Where history meets the future

Estonia

Estonia was an unexpected contrast for me. Walking through the cobblestone streets of Tallinn’s Old Town felt like stepping straight into a medieval fairytale.

Yet, just a little further along, you realize that Estonia is also known for its advanced digital society and thriving startup scene. It is fascinating how this small nation has embraced the future while preserving so much of its history.

The chilly sea breeze and the quiet, disciplined atmosphere left a lasting impression on me. Estonia reminded me that progress does not have to mean leaving your roots behind.

A feeling of its own

Turkey

To be honest, Turkey felt a little more special to me than some of the other countries I visited. Maybe it was the rich history, the coastal atmosphere, the tourists, or simply the weather.

Of course, every country has its own history, culture, and mentality. But for some reason, Turkey just hits different. There is something about its atmosphere that stays with you.
